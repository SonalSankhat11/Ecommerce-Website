Origins

The Atelier’s Vision

TRENDCART was established in 2018 inside a studio on C.G. Road, Ahmedabad. The goal was simple: create an alternative to rapid, low-quality apparel consumption by crafting garments that focus on durability, texture, and tailored fit.

We believe that a wardrobe shouldn't contain hundreds of fleeting trends. Instead, it should host a small number of versatile, beautifully crafted foundation pieces that are worn for years.

01

Sustainably Minded

We use GOTS-certified organic cotton, cruelty-free wools, and low-impact dyes, committing to zero plastic packaging by 2027.

02

Atelier Craftsmanship

Every sample is pattern-drafted in-house. Our manufacturing partners are selected based on living wage metrics and artisan legacies.

03

Radical Transparency

We share our pricing markup structure and detail the exact mill location where each yarn was spun, listed in our catalog.
